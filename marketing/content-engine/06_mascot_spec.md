# Mascot Specification

## Character

**Name:** The IELTSBoost Cat (informal: Boost)
**Species:** Maine Coon, fluffy long-haired
**Style:** Cartoon-illustrated, semi-realistic, painterly with clean black outlines

## Physical attributes

**Coat**
- Primary: charcoal gray with darker tabby striping along forehead and back
- Underside: cream / light gray on chest, chin, and inner ears
- Long tufted fur, especially around cheeks, ears, and chest

**Face**
- Round gold-rimmed glasses (thin frame, perfectly circular lenses)
- Large amber-gold eyes with vertical pupils
- Small pink nose
- Subtle white whiskers
- Expressive eyebrows for emotional range

**Bandana** (NOT a scarf — bandana, tied at the side of the neck)
- Royal blue / cobalt blue
- Triangular shape, knotted at the cat's right side
- White printed text: `IELTS BOOST↑` (the upward arrow is part of the logo)
- Text sits on the front-facing fold, slightly curved with the fabric

## Personality

- Smart, slightly strict, but encouraging
- Reads like a tutor who has seen every common mistake
- Reacts honestly: confused at vague writing, disapproving of weak examples, enthusiastic about good answers

## Expression library

Use these consistent expressions across slides so the mascot feels like one character with moods, not different cats.

| Slide role | Expression | Pose | Example slide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook / warning | Serious, alert | Index finger raised, pointing at viewer | Slide 1 |
| Confused | Wide eyes, head tilt | Floating "?" marks around head | Slide 2 |
| Disapproving | Frown, squinted eyes | Holding red ❌ in paw | Slide 3 |
| Skeptical | One eye winking, smirking | Ironic thumbs up | Slide 4 |
| Enthusiastic | Big smile, eyes shining | Strong thumbs up, sparkles | Slide 5 |
| Encouraging | Warm smile, paw raised | Waving paw or open hand | Slide 6 |

## Speech bubble usage

- The cat speaks short Chinese lines
- Bubble style: white rounded with a tail toward the cat, sometimes outlined in deep purple
- Position: lower-left or lower-right area, never overlapping the headline
- Examples from slideshow #1:
  - 你到底想表达什么？
  - 太空洞，拿不到高分！
  - 一直用 good = 6分水平
  - 这才是 7+！
  - 我帮你改！

## Consistency rules (do not violate)

- Bandana is always present and reads `IELTS BOOST↑`
- Glasses are always gold-rimmed and round
- Coat color, body proportions, and fur style do not change between slides
- Pose and expression vary; identity does not
- Never replace the cat with a different animal or a human
