## T6 — "Myth Busting"
*Hook: "这个说法是错的" | Structure: common belief → why it's wrong → what's true → what to do*

- [ ] **"用复杂词就能高分"** | Truth: examiners mark on precise fit, not length. One wrong complex word hurts more than one simple correct word.
- [ ] **"字数越多分数越高"** | Truth: Task 2 minimum is 250 words. Over 350 without quality = more errors exposed, not more marks.
- [ ] **"模板背得越多越好"** | Truth: examiners are trained to spot memorized chunks. Penalizes Lexical Resource.
- [ ] **"口语必须像母语者一样"** | Truth: IELTS Speaking is graded on intelligibility and fluency, not native accent. Heavy native-speaker mimicry can hurt if unnatural.
- [ ] **"雅思写作必须用名人名言"** | Truth: made-up quotes or irrelevant quotes hurt coherence. Examiners prefer logical reasoning over famous quotes.
- [ ] **"听力只要听懂大意就行"** | Truth: IELTS Listening tests specific words, numbers, and spellings. General comprehension is not enough.
